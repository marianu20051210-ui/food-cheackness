About FreshCheck

FreshCheck helps individuals and families prevent domestic food waste while safeguarding against foodborne illness.

💡 How It Works

  • Shelf Life Matrix: Calculates safe consumption windows from day of grocery purchase.
  • Visual Spoilage Engine: Analyzes pixel color saturation, dark spotting, and oxidation on uploaded food photos.
  • Zero Waste Storage Tips: Offers freezing, boiling, and composting suggestions.

Always trust your smell and sensory checks before eating. When in doubt, throw it out!
